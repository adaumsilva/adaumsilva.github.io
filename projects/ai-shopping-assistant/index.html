AI Shopping Assistant

A conversation that goes all the way to checkout.

PRODUCT GALLERY01 / 07

The storefront assistant helping a shopper choose a product, with add to cart and checkout inside the chat.

Overview

A production AI sales agent for WooCommerce, shipped as a WordPress plugin with a metered SaaS gateway. Shoppers can discover products in natural language, manage their cart, and check out in the conversation.

How it works

  • Product discovery through natural-language catalog search and OpenAI tool calling.
  • RAG-grounded answers from uploaded documents.
  • Human takeover from the admin inbox when a conversation needs personal attention.
